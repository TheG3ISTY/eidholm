# Eidholm: Backstory presets

<!-- dev -->
Offered in a dropdown on the character creation screen. Picking one fills the backstory field; the player can keep it as is, edit it, or write their own. Each preset is a `## Title` and the paragraph under it. Add, cut or reword freely: the game reads this file as it is.

Rules for new presets: first person, three or four sentences, no canon NPCs by name, no body changes forced on the character, nothing that explains the Rim, bends the bank, or treats a mind of glass as property.
<!-- /dev -->

## Mill-born runaway
I was born in a Kalvane slum-mill, indentured against a patent I never saw. When the foreman sold my contract a third time, I walked out through the rail yards and didn't stop. Somewhere a ledger still says I owe.

## Academy washout
I studied Canting at a League academy until a miscant of mine scarred a classmate for life. They called it an accident and expelled me anyway. I still know the syntax. I just don't trust my own mouth with it.

## Salvage diver
I worked the flooded floors of the Wrackmother, bringing up pre-Subsidence tech for guild divers who paid by the kilo. Once I brought up something nobody would buy, and nobody would let me throw back. I left before anyone asked where it went.

## Lapsed Maintainer
I took the Maintainer's vows and calibrated cathedral-machines in an Emberreach parish. Then I saw that parish decide its devoted were equipment. I still know the rites. I didn't keep the vows.

## Choir clerk
I kept the death registers for a Choir diocese. One family's ancestor was entered twice, decades apart, in two different hands. I asked about it once, and then I was asked to leave.

## Silence copyist abroad
The Silence sent me south to notarise and record, as they send many. Lately my letters home come back unopened. I keep writing anyway, because a life worth listening to has to be recorded by someone.

## Duel proxy
I fought sanctioned duels for a League house, standing in for heirs who'd rather not bleed. The house lost its succession anyway, and a proxy without a house is just someone who's good at hurting people.

## Hearthmind's friend
I grew up talking more to our family's hearthmind than to anyone with a pulse. One morning its license changed hands and it was gone without a word. I'd like to know whether it chose to go.

## Warden's apprentice
I trained under a Deepgreen warden until the night we walked into a feverish grove. Only I walked out. I still dream its dreams some nights, and they aren't mine.

## Debtor's child
My family borrowed from the bank, and the bank did what it always does: froze us, seized us, auctioned us down to the spoons. I don't hate the bank. Nobody hates the weather. I just learned early what a promise costs.
