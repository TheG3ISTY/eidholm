# Eidholm: Rules (Draft 0)

Status: **deliberately unresolved.** These get settled at the table, while playing.
Until a section is marked *Adopted*, the GM resolves things narratively and may propose
mechanics tagged `[PROVISIONAL]`. Anything the table likes gets written here and committed as a `rules:` change.

## Open questions

1. **Resolution mechanic.** Plain dice (d20, 2d6, dice pool)? Or something Lattice-flavoured, where a miscant is a distinct outcome from a simple failure?
2. **The Chamber spectrum as aptitude.** Is a character's position between Canting and Artifice their class, a stat, or a slider that moves over a career?
3. **Combat, wounds, scars.** How harm is tracked. Miscants scar per canon, so scars probably need to be a real, lasting thing.
4. **Progression.** XP, milestones, or growth through scars, debts and devotions.
5. **Currency and equipment.** The Concord pays in patents, not coin. What the other powers use, and how gear is valued.
