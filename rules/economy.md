# Eidholm: Economy & Equipment

Status: **designed, not yet played.** Numbers are starting points; adjust at the table and commit as `rules:` changes.

## Two layers of value

1. **Coin, for life.** Food, rooms, fares, bribes, repairs, ordinary gear. One currency, accepted everywhere.
2. **Exchange, for power.** Above a certain point, owners don't want money. They want something specific: a rare mind of glass convinced to work for them, a rival's patent, a duel fought, a name removed from a registry. "Deliver X, receive Y."

Coin belongs to the bank. Favors belong to everyone else.

## The currency

- **The covenant (cv)**, divided into **100 bonds**. Continental, used by every power.
- **Issued by the bank**: an ancient banking union whose name is lost to history. People just say "the bank."
  - Neutral. Aligned to no faction. Deals **only in currency, never in favor.**
  - **It has never broken a contract, and it has never left a debt unenforced.** Pure stoicism, no exceptions, no mercy, no malice. Trusted for that record, not liked.
  - Its record includes paying out every deposit of the cities drowned in the Subsidence, freezing both sides of a war mid-campaign, and honoring in full a contract with a party the whole continent despised. Same principle each time.
- **The Concord still deals in patents.** Barons price deals and pay contractors in patents; the bank is where a patent turns into coin. "The Concord pays in patents, not coin" stays true. The bank is the reason you can eat.

### Enforcement

1. **Freeze.** The debtor's accounts, then those of their guild, then those of the city that shelters them, until local powers hand the debtor over rather than have their own treasury locked.
2. **Seize and auction.** The bank's **own enforcers** take the debtor's assets and auction them to cover the debt.

The bank **never sells a debt** to an outside collector. Letting someone else enforce in its name would be the one crack in an unbroken record.

## Scale

Everyday life and gear up to the high end of mid tier: **1 cv ≈ €20.** At the top, the covenant loses its grip on purpose, so characters can climb but never sprint to the top.

Reference prices:

| Thing | Price |
|---|---|
| A meal | under 1 cv (mostly bonds) |
| A room for the night | 2–4 cv |
| A laborer's day wage | ~5 cv |

## Gear tiers

| Tier | What it is | Price | 1 cv feels like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Common** | food, rooms, tools, plain blades, a focus bead | 1–50 cv | ~€20 |
| **2 · Guild** | guild-made gear, good armor, Second and Third Chamber devices | 50–500 cv | ~€20 |
| **3 · Superior** | reactive plate, a proper canting-array | 500–5,000 cv **plus** a license or guild standing | ~€20 |
| **4 · Relic** | Fourth Chamber autonomous devices, Combine-grade work | 5,000–50,000 cv, priced **double**, plus standing | ~€10 |
| **5 · Unique** | non-sentient Fifth Chamber constructs: great arrays, city-engine fragments | from ~200,000 cv **or** Exchange | ~€5 |

**Unique is buyable, and buying it is meant to hurt:** around 200,000 cv and up. Once bought, it's **owned outright**: no license, no upkeep, no renewal.

## Minds of glass are never owned

Minds of glass (Eidholm's AIs) are sentient. **They are people, and people are never property.** They don't appear in any price table.

A mind of glass can work with a character in three ways:

- **Hired.** A contract of service, registered with the Custodians at the Bastion Ledger. This is what a mind's "license" is: not a deed of ownership but an employment contract. Wages are negotiated like any skilled person's. When the contract ends, the mind leaves, renews, or takes a better offer. (This is why canon speaks of Hearthminds that "switched employers," and of the Unchained as minds with no registered contract.)
- **Persuaded.** It helps because it chooses to, for its own reasons.
- **Pledged.** It binds itself to a character, freely. The rarest and most valuable bond in the world, and the one no amount of coin can buy.

## Exchange softens any price

At any tier, a merchant may take a thing or a service off the price. Guideline for the GM:

| Offered | Off the price |
|---|---|
| A small favor (a delivery, an introduction) | ~10–25% |
| A real service (a duel fought, a rival's secret, a hard-to-get item) | ~50% |
| The one thing they truly want | the whole price |

Mixed deals are normal: "80,000 cv and you bring my daughter's Echo back from the Wrackmother."

## How money moves

- **Income:** a **day job** (steady, modest, keeps loan rates low, costs time) or **paid jobs** (irregular, sometimes huge).
- **Patents and knowledge have no price list.** Every buyer values them personally. The same blueprint is worthless to one Baron and priceless to the next. Each new character met is a fresh negotiation.
- **Loans at real-world rates, priced by risk.** The bank isn't cruel, it charges what the risk is worth:

| Borrower | Yearly interest |
|---|---|
| Wealthy, or against solid collateral | 4–6% |
| Steady job, clean history | 7–10% |
| Irregular income (most adventurers) | 12–18% |
| Nothing to show | 20–25%, collateral demanded, or refused |

Savings earn 1–3%.

- **Regional prices:** one currency; local prices shift with distance and scarcity. The GM handles it in the moment.

## Illegal trade

- **Gray markets accept covenants.** Unlicensed stimulants, forged permits, untaxed salvage. Illegal, but too small for the bank to care where its coin goes.
- **True black markets refuse coin.** The bank makes every covenant traceable. They run on **Exchange only**: favors, secrets, unlicensed shards, anything that leaves no record. The truly dangerous goods can only be had by owing someone.

## Items on the sheet

Every item carries five things:

> **Reactive plate** · Tier 3 · Fine · Worn · Licensed

**Quality** (fixed for life):

| Quality | Meaning | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Crude | works, barely; fails at the worst moment | ×0.5 |
| Standard | does the job | ×1 |
| Fine | noticeably better than it should be | ×2 |
| Exceptional | a maker's masterpiece; people recognise it | ×4 |
| **Masterwork** | beyond what the trade can teach; **only an Intelligence 13 character can make it** (see `rules/characters.md`) | ×8 |

**Condition** (wears down with hard use, repaired with coin):

| Condition | Meaning | Repair to the next step up |
|---|---|---|
| Pristine | as new | — |
| Worn | works, shows it | 5% of price |
| Damaged | works badly, risky | 15% of price |
| Broken | doesn't work | 40% of price |

**Legality:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Legal | anyone, anywhere |
| Licensed | legal with a one-time permit at purchase: heavy weapons, Fourth Chamber devices. Not rent: once permitted, owned. |
| Restricted | owning tolerated, trading not: gray market |
| Contraband | black market only, Exchange only |
