# Eidholm: Spells

Status: **adopted.** The server reads the named spells straight from the tables below (name, tier, and whether the roll is Ranged canting), so editing a table here changes the game.

## How the spellbook works

- **Named protocols** at every tier from 1 to 5: taught, reliable, written down in academies and guild manuals.
- **Freeform cants** built from the root-language grammar: anything the caster can phrase, priced by the tier its effect reaches. Riskier than a protocol.
- **Tier 6 has no list at all.** See below.

Costs and limits are in `rules/characters.md` (pool, recovery, highest tier unaided, miscants).

### Learning spells

A canter can only cast the named spells they know. Spells are learned three ways:

- **Taught:** by a teacher, an academy, a guild, a diocese. Usually for coin, service or standing.
- **Found:** from manuals, etched beads, salvaged arrays, a dead canter's notes.
- **By doing:** working it out from theory or from watching it cast, then trying it.

Every spell the canter knows goes on their sheet. Until it has been **cast successfully once**, it is **unproven**.

### Miscants

| What is being cast | Miscants on a natural |
|---|---|
| A **proven** spell (cast successfully before) | 1 |
| An **unproven** spell (learned, never yet cast successfully) | 1 or 2 |
| A **freeform** cant | 1 or 2 |
| A **first fusion** at tier 6 | 1, 2 or 3 |

Luck 13 removes only the natural 1, never the rest. Backlash always follows the tier (see `rules/characters.md`).

Every spell rolls **Resonance + Canting** (or **Ranged canting** for anything thrown at a target), against a DC or the target's AC or save.

## Tier 6: world-altering

There are no standard tier-6 spells. Only a canter with **Resonance 13** can cast at this tier, and every one of them **writes their own**.

### Writing one

Every tier-6 working is written by its caster, in play. Two ways:

- **Developed:** composed from nothing in the root-language during downtime, about **a week of in-game time**. Anything within the scale below.
- **Fused:** the fast road. Weave **two tier-5 spells or three tier-4 spells** into one working that does what its parts do, at tier-6 scale (Ashfall + Quake becomes a burning collapse across a whole city quarter). Custom spellweaving at its peak.
  - Fusion happens **on the spot**: the weaving is the cast, mid-fight if need be, for the usual 39 Resonance.
  - A first fusion is improvisation at the edge of what a mind can hold: it **miscants on a natural 1, 2 or 3** (Luck 13 removes only the 1). Once it has worked, it is a named working like any other and miscants on a 1.
- Either way the result is theirs: named, on their sheet, castable again. The player states in one sentence what it does; the GM checks it against the benchmarks and accepts it, or says where it reaches too far.

### The scale

Tier 6 is Fifth Chamber power from a single mind, in combat and out of it. Benchmarks:

- Lift a city block and set it down elsewhere (what the Corso Bell's arrays do).
- Turn the weather over a region for a day.
- Raise a wall across a valley, or open a pass through a mountain.
- Sink a fleet, or lift one off the seabed.
- Strip every lattice-shield on a battlefield.
- Silence the Lattice across a whole city for an hour.

### The price

- **39 Resonance** per cast: one working, then hours of recovery.
- **Every cast is felt** across the continent as a tremor in the Lattice. Custodians, Barons, the Choir and the Unchained all notice, and some come asking.

### What it can never do

- **Undo death.** No resurrection. Decommissioning is final.
- **Bend a mind of glass.** No compelling, rewriting or owning a sapient mind. They are people.
- **Break the bank.** No erasing debts, forging covenants or touching the ledger.
- **The Rim** can be reached, but reaching it is not reading it. What comes back is in a language and encoding no one understands, and the GM never explains it.
