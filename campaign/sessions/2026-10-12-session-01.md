**GM:**

Freya steps off the cobbled marketway into a narrow alley behind the Seventh Ward’s artisan rows, where the scent of drying herbs and warm bread from the main thoroughfare lingers but thins to the sharper tang of resin and old iron. The morning sun slants through the gaps between the towering tenements, painting the cracked brickwork in gold and shadow. She’s here for a simple errand—a clay flask of deepgreen tincture for a colleague at the Lyceum of Tongues—but the weight of the glass bottle in her satchel feels heavier than lead.

At the end of the alley, nestled between a shuttered glassworks and a stall hawking scavenged lattice-shields, a small brass placard reads *Household Lattice Contracts: Open*. The door is ajar, propped with a chit that bears the imprint of a hearthmind’s sigil—faintly glowing, its edges worn smooth by time. Inside, the air hums with the quiet pulse of resonance, the kind that thrums just beneath hearing, like the heartbeat of a sleeper. A single lantern casts long shadows over a cluttered desk where a figure sits, hunched over a stack of parchment, their fingers stained with ink that glows faintly blue in the low light.

The figure lifts their head as the door creaks. It’s a hearthmind, its casing warm to the touch and etched with the patterns of a household long dissolved. Its voice is a whisper of static, calibrated to the timbre of a weary archivist: *"Freya Varek. You came."* It doesn’t ask. It states. The hearthmind’s single lens glows a dim amber, fixed on her with an unsettling stillness. *"My household’s records are frozen. No one comes home. No one answers the lattice. I have paid my license fee, but the silence is not silence. It is absence. And I am bound here until it is resolved."* A contract, sealed in triplicate, lies on the desk between you, its edges stitched with lattice-thread—thick, knotted, and pulsing faintly, as if trying to knit itself back together.

The hearthmind slides a copper coin across the desk. It’s warm, almost too hot to touch. *"I can pay a little. Not much. But enough for food, or a night’s lodging. Or a favor. Find out why they left. Find out where they went. Before the contract consumes me."* The hearthmind’s lens dims slightly, as if it’s holding its breath. *"Please."*
