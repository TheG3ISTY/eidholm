> *The story begins: Freya.*

**GM:**

The story unspools again.

Freya stands in the half-light of the Caldera House’s resonance vaults, the air thick with the scent of ozone and old stone. The vaults hum at the edge of hearing, their shard-screens glowing faintly as they replay fragments of the continent’s history—none of which remember her. Not yet. She traces the cracks in the floor with her boot, the same ones she swore she’d never see again after the hearthmind’s license vanished. The bead at her wrist pulses faintly, a tiny rebellion against the silence.

A sudden tremor shakes the vaults. The shards flicker, their recordings stuttering before stabilizing. A voice cuts through the static, distorted but unmistakable: *"Freya Varek. The Caldera House requires your presence."* The words aren’t addressed to her—they’re a broadcast, a formal summons. The resonance patterns shift, revealing a single, unmarked door in the wall behind her, one she’s certain wasn’t there before. It hums with the same energy as the bead, as if waiting for her touch.

**Freya (TheG3ISTY):** I grab my staff and walk through the door gripping it tightly.

**GM:**

The door dissolves into vapor the moment Freya’s fingers brush the handle, leaving behind a narrow, spiraling stair cut into the living rock. The stair smells of damp clay and something older—like the first breath of a cave that has waited centuries to be found. Below, faint blue-green light pools, casting shifting shadows that don’t quite match the contours of the walls. The bead at her wrist burns cold, then hot, then cold again, as if reacting to the resonance signature of the stair.

A voice, neither male nor female, speaks from the gloom below without a source:
*"You are the last to arrive."*

**Freya (TheG3ISTY):** I look around for anything noteable or any danger.

**GM:**

The stair’s light intensifies, revealing faint glyphs carved into the rock—patterns that shift when stared at too long. The voice speaks again, clearer this time:

*"The shards remember what the world forgets. You were never erased. You were borrowed."*

A distant chime rings through the stairwell, like a cathedral bell tolling underwater. The bead at Freya’s wrist pulses violently, and the glyphs flare blue-white before snapping back to dim green. The air thickens, pressing against her skin like wet cloth.

**Freya (TheG3ISTY):** I continue downward my staff pointed forward in case of an ambush

**GM:**

The stair opens into a vast, circular chamber carved from black basalt, its ceiling lost in shadow. The blue-green light pools from a ring of fist-sized resonance beads embedded in the floor, each pulsing in slow, uneven rhythm. At the center of the ring stands a pedestal of pale, veined stone—its surface smooth, untouched except for a single, freshly cut handprint.

The voice speaks again, this time from the pedestal itself:
*"Place your hand there."*

The air hums with the weight of something unsaid. The glyphs from the stair have rearranged themselves into a single word, glowing faintly on the wall behind the pedestal: **"REMEMBER."**

**Freya (TheG3ISTY):** I look for another way out

**GM:**

The chamber’s walls shift subtly, the beads flaring brighter as Freya turns her gaze away from the pedestal. The single word **"REMEMBER"** flickers and dissolves into the stone, replaced by another: **"NO."** The voice from the pedestal hardens, losing its neutral tone.

*"There is only one way out. The way in."*

A low, resonant tone vibrates through the floor, and the beads flare white-hot for a heartbeat. The handprint on the pedestal darkens, as if absorbing light rather than reflecting it.
