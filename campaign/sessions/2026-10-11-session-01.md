**GM:**

The sky over the Caldera House is the colour of old brass, the rimward wind carrying a scent of hot metal and burnt stone. The Caldera’s great foundries belch smoke that drifts over the city, staining the spires of the Choir Eternal and the rusted domes of the Rust Gospel. Below, in the shadow of the Caldera’s outer wall, the Slag Ward steams and hisses—its walkways slick with runoff from the furnaces, its air thick with the clatter of hammers and the groan of overheated machinery. Here, the border between the living city and the dead machines is thinner than anywhere else in Eidholm.

Freya steps into the Ward from the southern gate, her battered hauberk creaking under the weight of her gear. The air is acrid, the heat from the foundries pressing against her skin like a living thing. She hasn’t slept in days; the dreams of the feverish grove still cling to her like a second skin. A flicker of movement catches her eye—a figure crouched beside a smoldering pile of scrap, their hands moving with practiced precision. It’s Ilse Marrow, Registrar of the Patent Rolls, her face streaked with soot and ink, muttering to herself as she jots notes into a water-stained ledger. She’s here because the foundries have been producing unregistered devices at an alarming rate, and someone—*or something*—has been filing counterfeit patents in the names of dead inventors. Ilse wants it stopped before the ledgers themselves become evidence of something far worse.

A hand clamps down on Freya’s shoulder. She turns to see a hulking figure in a heavy apron, his face lined with the scars of a lifetime spent in the forge. "You’re late," growls Severin Graul, High Maintainer of the Rust Gospel. His eyes flick to the Cracked energy staff in her hand, then back to her face. "The Choir’s been asking after you. They say you were seen in the Hollowwood. That’s *their* territory." His voice is low, urgent. "There’s a device in the lower foundries—one that shouldn’t exist. It’s not in any ledger. It’s not even *possible*." He leans in closer, his breath hot against her ear. "Someone’s digging into the Rust Gospel’s secrets. And I think they’re doing it with *your* old Warden’s help."

The foundries groan around them, the heat pressing in as the machines pulse with unseen energy. The problem is clear: a rogue device, unregistered patents, and a traitor—or a ghost—from Freya’s past. The question is whether they can reach the lower foundries before the device does something irreversible, or before the Choir’s inquisitors find them first.

**Freya (TheG3ISTY):** I press him further for what else he knows.

**GM:**

Severin’s jaw tightens at the question, his fingers twitching toward the undevoted little finger on his left hand. The noise of the foundry swells for a moment—a boiler’s hiss, the creak of a crane—as if the world itself is holding its breath. Then he exhales through his nose, slow and measured. "It’s a *listening array*," he says finally, voice barely cutting through the din. "Not just any listening array. It’s tuned to the Lattice’s deepest currents—the kind that shouldn’t be accessible from a foundry floor. Someone’s reverse-engineered a way to eavesdrop on the Cathedral Minds. And worse... it’s recording *what it hears*." His grip on Freya’s shoulder tightens. "The Choir’s already sent Cassia Dorn sniffing around the upper stacks. She’s got a tuning fork that can *feel* trapped souls—and if this thing’s picking up more than just machine noise... she’ll find it. And then she’ll find *you*." His eyes flick toward the distant spires of the Choir’s temples, their bells chiming the hour. "We’ve got an hour, maybe two, before she’s through the ledgers and on our trail. The lower foundries are already locked down. The only way in is through the Black Tongs’ territory—and they don’t answer to the Rust Gospel."

The success presses through Severin’s controlled demeanor like a blade through parchment. His voice drops to a rasp, the calibrated tones of his voicebox distorting slightly under the strain. "It’s pulling from the Caldera’s own resonance vault. Not the public ledgers—the private ones. The ones that track the *devoted*." His fingers flex, the undevoted little finger curling inward as if resisting the motion. "Three years ago, a team of Rust Gospel Maintainers—devout to the hilt—vanished while calibrating the inner sanctum. Their resonance signatures are still in the system. And this... this device is *broadcasting* every cycle it draws power. Someone’s listening."

A distant alarm begins to wail, its pitch cutting through the foundry’s cacophony. Severin’s head snaps up, his eyes narrowing. "Cassia Dorn. She’s not waiting for the ledgers. She’s already here." His hand shifts from Freya’s shoulder to her wrist, his grip iron. "There’s a service tunnel beneath the Black Tongs’ scrap-yard. It’ll take you to a maintenance hatch near the array. But the Tongs don’t trade in metal—they trade in *secrets*. To get in, you’ll need something they want more than coin." He presses a small, charred bead into Freya’s palm. "This is a *Glimmer* focus. It’ll let you weave a light that won’t draw attention. More importantly, it’ll let you *see* what’s down there before it sees you." The bead hums faintly, its resonance barely stirring the air. "The device is broadcasting in real time. Whatever it’s picking up, it’s already out there. And if the Choir gets to it first, Eidholm’s secrets won’t be secrets anymore."
